# Wan 2.2 14B roadmap

Last reviewed: **2026-08-13**
Target workstation: **Windows 11, RTX 5080 16 GB (SM120), Python 3.12**

## Executive decision

Wan 2.2 14B is not one generic checkpoint. Its A14B video lines use a two-expert
Mixture-of-Experts denoising topology:

1. a **high-noise expert** for the early denoising stage;
2. a **low-noise expert** for the later stage;
3. separate official model lines for **T2V** and **I2V**.

Engine currently implements I2V through an exact five-resource Comfy-aligned
stored-FP8 closure. The current-template Euler/simple path has passed a narrow
target-workstation lifecycle baseline and is the current **Fallback** rather than
Recommended: it is the exact broadly-compatible Comfy FP8 path, while corpus,
source-invalidation, peer-switch, and reference-quality evidence remain incomplete.
The support-only directory is an automatically acquirable, immutable 12-file Hugging
Face whitelist.

Do not broaden into T2V, first/last-frame, arbitrary community acceleration LoRAs, or
unqualified quantized formats. The accepted fallback now has one separately accepted
I2V challenger—the official Comfy LightX2V four-step LoRA pair. Winnougan INT8
ConvRot artifacts are cataloged with immutable source and header facts, but remain
non-runnable: their available community sample is not a clean operation authority.
The small ladder is matching dense BF16 I2V as reference, the current exact scaled-FP8
I2V path as fallback, and separately qualified challengers. T2V now has a separately
typed, exact-current-template implementation seam awaiting target-workstation
acceptance; it is not inferred from I2V conditioning or results.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified** — stated by Wan, Comfy, or the Engine source/catalog at the audited
  commit.
- **Publisher measurement** — an upstream speed/memory claim, not an Engine result.
- **Inference** — a roadmap product judgment requiring target-workstation validation.

active parameters per denoising step, supporting 480p and 720p. The model weights are
Apache-2.0. Verified sources:

## Current Engine implementation

- **Package-owned I2V recipe exists:**
  [`wan-2-2-14b-i2v-image-to-video-comfy-org-fp8.toml`](../../src/latentslate_engine/builtin_recipes/wan22/wan-2-2-14b-i2v-image-to-video-comfy-org-fp8.toml).
- **Native stored-weight runtime exists:**
  [`tools/wan22_native.py`](../../src/latentslate_engine/tools/wan22_native.py)
  and the associated runtime implement I2V only, without runtime weight conversion.
- **Default Engine operation:** 16 fps, 81 frames, **640×640**, 20 steps, high/low CFG
  3.5, `comfy_split`, **Euler/simple**, and shift 5. The built-in recipe fixes the
  sampler/stage/CFG semantics from the active Comfy graph; dimensions, frames, prompt,
  and seed remain explicit supported inputs. Cancellation/failure terminates a
  disposable worker process rather than trusting in-process allocator cleanup. A
  different sampler/schedule is a separately qualified alternate recipe, not a hidden
  variation of this one.
- **Acquisition is exact and remote provisionable.** The support declaration pins the
  official revision and enumerates 12 exact `allow_patterns`, whose aggregate is
  529,069,044 bytes. The whitelist includes only the required configs/tokenizer,
  `README.md`, and the official Diffusers VAE; no transformer or text-encoder weight
  path can be selected.
- **Pre-parity target-workstation denoise result:** the earlier 832×480, 81-frame, 20-step
  (10+10 stage split), CFG 3.5/3.5, seed `43301611940728` baseline completed on the
  RTX 5080. Client time was 1,297.74 s (server execution 1,297.42 s); device-wide
  sampled VRAM peaked at 14,584 MiB (100% utilization). The original 24-fps muxed MP4
  is valid pre-parity stage/VRAM/source-conditioned output evidence but is **not**
  sampler-parity acceptance. The exact retained 81-frame H.264 bitstream was losslessly timestamp-remuxed
  with `ffmpeg -itsscale 1.5 -c copy` to 16 fps / 5.041667 s (191,267 bytes, SHA-256
  `5948f6aa0473983513381f8c885868d55cc74e5043e5f276f13fdfcc381b0e4e`), preserving
  generation evidence without rerunning inference. The older shift-8 adapter is not
  current-template parity. Initial teardown exposed unreleased CPU-resident tensors;
  explicit Python-level dematerialization did **not** release the Windows process heap.
  A historical shift-8 run completed:
  1,282.08 s client / 1,281.09 s server, 14,895 MiB device-wide sampled peak (100%),
  and a 199,789-byte 832×480 H.264 MP4 with 81 frames at 16 fps / 5.0625 s, SHA-256
  `b063589f05c294c6e8f1994b28576442ed0aced5ea05d41d9e0397d7b05e2832`. It records
  `pipeline_warm=false` and `materialized_fresh`, but its wrapper-only unload was later
  disproved by host-memory telemetry. Visual smoke review retained coherent source
  conditioning. It is historical evidence only, not current-template parity.
- **Current-template target-workstation baseline (2026-08-13 UTC):** an isolated Engine
  on the RTX 5080 completed the exact 640×640 / 81-frame / 16-fps I2V contract with
  seed `43301611940728`, 20 split 10/10 steps, CFG 3.5/3.5, Euler/simple, and shift 5.
  Server execution was **1,325.720 s**. The retained 166,319-byte H.264 MP4 is
  640×640, 16 fps, 81 frames, and 5.0625 s; SHA-256
  `83065c74cadadcba1249ff02fd2ffbe3f4f401bf9ebd2421db9eef5c3b12f665`.
  The disposable worker (PID 49476) exited before success; its tree was absent and the
  parent returned to its bounded post-job host-memory state. A materialization-time
  cancellation then became terminal only after its worker/tree had exited, produced no
  artifact or owned encoder temporary, and returned the parent private bytes from
  1,767,239,680 to 1,758,081,024. A fresh recovery worker (PID 53704) succeeded in
  **1,273.441 s**, exited cleanly, and produced the byte-identical MP4; parent private
  bytes changed only from 1,758,081,024 to 1,762,353,152. Ignored study manifests and

  120-second cap interrupted only the first harness client, not the Engine job; final
  evidence was reconstructed from the public API and downloaded artifact, and is not
  represented as a harness-complete run. This narrow baseline is not a promotion to
  Recommended.
- **T2V and first/last are absent.** No native Engine recipe/runtime exists for those
  operations.
- **LightX2V is a separate experimental recipe:**
  [`wan-2-2-14b-i2v-image-to-video-comfy-org-fp8-lightx2v-4step.toml`](../../src/latentslate_engine/builtin_recipes/wan22/wan-2-2-14b-i2v-image-to-video-comfy-org-fp8-lightx2v-4step.toml)

  and 16 fps. The pair is declared from
  `Comfy-Org/Wan_2.2_ComfyUI_Repackaged@fb1388adc906ab39ffc26ee40e96b22886b56bc4`:
  high `wan2.2_i2v_lightx2v_4steps_lora_v1_high_noise.safetensors`
  (1,226,977,424 bytes, SHA-256
  `d176c808d6fc461999b68e321efcb7501b20b8c3797523ed0df14f7d1deff11e`) and low
  `wan2.2_i2v_lightx2v_4steps_lora_v1_low_noise.safetensors`

  `024f21de095bc8fad9809ded3e9e49a2e170dcf27075da8145ba7d60d8aab7f9`).

  not use that bypass. **Target-workstation
  acceptance (2026-08-13 UTC):** the isolated RTX 5080 Engine completed the exact

  `5b1f39d1df5e159e7e67d1c4eae9675b8814ab8491fde3d3af1ba550bfb88839`.

## Small qualification ladder

### I2V

1. **Reference:** exact official dense BF16 I2V-A14B repository, 20 steps, fixed
   high/low boundary, CFG, source image, seed, dimensions, frames, and 16 fps.
2. **Fallback incumbent:** the current exact five-resource stored-FP8 Engine path with
   the same operation settings.
3. **Experimental challengers:** official Comfy LightX2V I2V is accepted as its own
   fixed four-step paired-LoRA operation. Winnougan INT8 ConvRot is cataloged as a
   separate investigation but has no recipe until a clean active workflow and exact
   operation contract are pinned. Neither alters the baseline recipe's fixed
   sampler/schedule.

The BF16 reference may require more host RAM/offload than is product-viable; it still
must be retained for output comparison. A reference is not automatically the default.

### T2V

Deferred. When product demand exists, create a separate BF16 versus official Comfy FP8
ladder using T2V-specific high/low experts. Never reuse an I2V transformer contract.

## Model-specific acceptance

Use the shared harness in [README](./README.md), plus an I2V corpus covering:

- people, faces, full-body articulation, animals, vehicles, products, landscapes, and
  stylized illustration;
- static camera, pan, tilt, dolly, orbit, handheld, and subject-driven motion;
- low-motion and high-motion prompts, source-image text/signage, fine texture, and
  occlusion/reappearance;
- 480p and 720p-class buckets, 81 and 121 frames, with playback fixed at 16 fps;
- prompt adherence versus source-image fidelity, first-frame reconstruction, identity,
  anatomy, motion smoothness, temporal texture, and camera realism.

Record source-image preprocessing, UMT5 encode/cache, high-expert load and steps,
stage boundary/ejection, low-expert load and steps, VAE decode, mux/export, peak
VRAM/RAM, disk reads, and host-device transfer. Exercise cancellation during each
expert load, each denoising stage, the stage transition, VAE decode, and export; then
run a clean follow-up job.

Measure warm reuse both with and without reusing the same source/prompt. A session that
keeps both 14B experts or stale latent state in memory is a failure even if the first
job succeeds.

## Hard gaps and unresolved source conflicts

1. **Qualification gap:** the exact fallback baseline plus LightX success, denoise
   cancellation, and fresh recovery are recorded; corpus coverage, changed-source,
   peer switching, and dense-reference quality comparison are not. These recipes
   intentionally have no warm execution cache: retaining both materialized 14B experts
   and UMT5 on the 64 GB host would leave unsafe RAM headroom, so repeated calls are
   measured as rebuilds instead.
2. **Reference feasibility:** dense BF16 may not be practical on 16 GB, but no quantized
   output can be accepted without a matching I2V reference somewhere in the harness.
3. **Stage topology is semantic:** high and low experts are not interchangeable files,
   and the stage boundary is part of the recipe.
4. **T2V is separate:** its 16-channel `WanPipeline` operation has a typed Engine
   seam and exact pinned closure, but I2V proof says nothing about T2V output quality,
   staged residency, cancellation, or lifecycle until its own target-workstation run.
5. **ConvRot operation gate:** the three Winnougan artifacts have immutable source,
   size, hash, and independently inspected header/schema facts. Existing Wan and
   UMT5 stored adapters have generic `comfy_quant/int8_tensorwise_convrot` coverage,
   but the exact three remote headers were not fed to their planners: bounded header
   retrieval was unavailable and the multi-GiB payloads were intentionally not
   downloaded. Exact-artifact planning and native dispatch therefore remain pending.
   The only available sample embeds custom attention/VSR nodes, official LightX plus
   additional unpinned LoRAs, and a different four-step operation. It is evidence
   that the files ran in Comfy, not a reproducible Engine recipe authority.

## Explicit non-goals

- Do not merge T2V and I2V artifacts or acceptance results.
- Do not treat one 14B expert as a complete model.
- Do not add further quantization formats beyond the two bounded challengers before
  the exact FP8 baseline has broader creator-value and reference qualification.
- Do not rebuild or quantize official weights at Engine runtime.
- Do not change Wan 14B I2V playback rate inside the recipe; use a downstream
  interpolation/retiming operation when a different delivery rate is wanted.
- Do not call local manual artifact presence a reproducible deployment lock.
