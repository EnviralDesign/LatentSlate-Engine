# Wan 2.2 14B roadmap

Last reviewed: **2026-08-13**
Target workstation: **Windows 11, RTX 5080 16 GB (SM120), Python 3.12**

## Executive decision

Wan 2.2 14B is not one generic checkpoint. Its A14B video lines use a two-expert
Mixture-of-Experts denoising topology:

1. a **high-noise expert** for the early denoising stage;
2. a **low-noise expert** for the later stage;
3. separate official model lines for **T2V** and **I2V**.

Engine currently implements I2V through an exact five-resource Comfy-aligned
stored-FP8 closure. The current-template Euler/simple path has passed a narrow
target-workstation lifecycle baseline and is the current **Fallback** rather than
Recommended: it is the exact broadly-compatible Comfy FP8 path, while corpus,
source-invalidation, peer-switch, and reference-quality evidence remain incomplete.
The support-only directory is an automatically acquirable, immutable 12-file Hugging
Face whitelist.

Do not broaden beyond the staged official first/last-frame baseline, arbitrary community
acceleration LoRAs, or unqualified quantized formats. The accepted fallbacks now have
separately accepted I2V and T2V challengers—the official Comfy LightX2V four-step LoRA
pairs. Winnougan INT8 ConvRot artifacts are cataloged with immutable source and header
facts, but remain non-runnable: their available community sample is not a clean
operation authority.
The small ladder is matching dense BF16 I2V as reference, the current exact scaled-FP8
I2V path as fallback, and separately qualified challengers. T2V has its own separately
typed exact-current-template fallback and Experimental LightX acceptance; neither is
inferred from I2V conditioning or results.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified** — stated by Wan, Comfy, or the Engine source/catalog at the audited
  commit.
- **Publisher measurement** — an upstream speed/memory claim, not an Engine result.
- **Inference** — a roadmap product judgment requiring target-workstation validation.

## Scope and lineage boundaries

| Line | Operation | Upstream topology | Engine state | Comparison boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wan 2.2 T2V-A14B | Text-to-video | High-noise + low-noise 14B experts, UMT5, VAE, scheduler/tokenizer support | Exact current-template FP8 baseline accepted as Fallback | Separate BF16/FP8 T2V ladder; never share I2V conditioning |
| Wan 2.2 I2V-A14B | Image-to-video | High-noise + low-noise 14B experts, UMT5, Wan 2.1 VAE, I2V support/config | Exact Engine path exists | Current roadmap priority |
| First/last-frame or other control variants | Additional conditioning or community workflows | Official FLF is a separate accepted Fallback operation; other controls remain distinct | Exact two-endpoint contract accepted on one RTX 5080 | Never infer behavior from ordinary I2V |

Wan's official model card describes A14B as a roughly 27B total MoE with about 14B
active parameters per denoising step, supporting 480p and 720p. The model weights are
Apache-2.0. Verified sources:
[official Wan2.2 code](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2/tree/42bf4cfaa384bc21833865abc2f9e6c0e67233dc),
[official I2V-A14B model card](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-I2V-A14B), and
[official I2V Diffusers repository](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-I2V-A14B-Diffusers).

## Official Comfy topology and settings

The official Comfy I2V example uses:

- `wan2.2_i2v_high_noise_14B_fp8_scaled.safetensors`;
- `wan2.2_i2v_low_noise_14B_fp8_scaled.safetensors`;
- scaled-FP8 UMT5-XXL;
- `wan_2.1_vae.safetensors`;
- 20 steps split at step 10;
- CFG 3.5 for both stages;
- Euler sampler, `simple` scheduler, and model-sampling shift 5.

Pinned active implementation reference: [workflow templates revision
`2b7f823136606344f0bccce249898d771b809aa1`,

template confirms its `CreateVideo` node is 16 fps. Engine fixes FPS at 16;

The active official Comfy T2V authority is
[`video_wan2_2_14B_t2v.json`](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/blob/2b7f823136606344f0bccce249898d771b809aa1/templates/video_wan2_2_14B_t2v.json)

noise latent, shift 5, 640×640 / 81 frames at 16 fps, Euler/simple, 20 steps split
10/10, and CFG 3.5 in both stages. The dedicated Engine T2V seam pins the two
T2V FP8 experts and a distinct 12-file `WanPipeline`/boundary-0.875 support snapshot
at `Wan-AI/Wan2.2-T2V-A14B-Diffusers@5be7df9619b54f4e2667b2755bc6a756675b5cd7`
(529,069,135 bytes); it rejects I2V's 36-channel expert and first-frame condition.

whose explicit enabled graph fixes the official T2V LightX2V v1.1 high/low pair at
strength 1, four Euler/simple steps split 2/2, CFG 1, shift 5, and 16 fps. Engine
catalogs that branch as a separate Experimental recipe; it is not a fallback-recipe
parameter. It has passed narrow target-workstation lifecycle acceptance, while broad
quality and reference comparison remain required for any tier change.

### I2V

1. **Reference:** exact official dense BF16 I2V-A14B repository, 20 steps, fixed
   high/low boundary, CFG, source image, seed, dimensions, frames, and 16 fps.
2. **Fallback incumbent:** the current exact five-resource stored-FP8 Engine path with
   the same operation settings.
3. **Experimental challengers:** official Comfy LightX2V I2V is accepted as its own
   fixed four-step paired-LoRA operation. Winnougan INT8 ConvRot is cataloged as a
   separate investigation but has no recipe until a clean active workflow and exact
   operation contract are pinned. Neither alters the baseline recipe's fixed
   sampler/schedule.

### First/last-frame I2V

1. **Reference:** a future dense I2V FLF operation with the same two-endpoint contract;
   ordinary I2V evidence cannot stand in for it.
2. **Fallback incumbent:** the corrected exact Comfy FP8 FLF operation: two endpoint
   images, 20 steps split 10/10, CFG 4, shift 8, Euler/simple, and 16 fps.
3. **Experimental challenger:** the pinned template's disabled LightX2V four-step FLF
   branch is future work. It must remain a separate operation and receive its own
   contract, dispatch proof, and target-workstation acceptance before exposure.

The BF16 reference may require more host RAM/offload than is product-viable; it still
must be retained for output comparison. A reference is not automatically the default.

### T2V

1. **Reference:** exact official dense BF16 T2V-A14B repository, retained for future
   output comparison on suitable hardware.
2. **Fallback incumbent:** the exact official Comfy FP8 T2V pair, with its separate
   16-channel `WanPipeline` support closure and fixed 20-step operation.
3. **Experimental challenger:** the separately pinned official LightX T2V paired-LoRA
   four-step operation, lifecycle-accepted but awaiting broader quality qualification.
   Never reuse an I2V transformer or conditioning contract.

## Model-specific acceptance

Use the shared harness in [README](./README.md), plus an I2V corpus covering:

- people, faces, full-body articulation, animals, vehicles, products, landscapes, and
  stylized illustration;
- static camera, pan, tilt, dolly, orbit, handheld, and subject-driven motion;
- low-motion and high-motion prompts, source-image text/signage, fine texture, and
  occlusion/reappearance;
- 480p and 720p-class buckets, 81 and 121 frames, with playback fixed at 16 fps;
- prompt adherence versus source-image fidelity, first-frame reconstruction, identity,
  anatomy, motion smoothness, temporal texture, and camera realism.

Record source-image preprocessing, UMT5 encode/cache, high-expert load and steps,
stage boundary/ejection, low-expert load and steps, VAE decode, mux/export, peak
VRAM/RAM, disk reads, and host-device transfer. Exercise cancellation during each
expert load, each denoising stage, the stage transition, VAE decode, and export; then
run a clean follow-up job.

Measure warm reuse both with and without reusing the same source/prompt. A session that
keeps both 14B experts or stale latent state in memory is a failure even if the first
job succeeds.

## Hard gaps and unresolved source conflicts

1. **Qualification gap:** the exact I2V, T2V, and FLF fallback baselines plus the I2V
   and T2V LightX successes, denoise cancellation, and fresh recovery are recorded;
   corpus coverage, changed-source, endpoint-pair diversity, peer switching, and
   dense-reference quality comparison are not. FLF's result is one pinned pair on one
   RTX 5080 with device-wide VRAM sampling. These recipes intentionally have no warm
   execution cache: retaining both materialized 14B experts and UMT5 on the 64 GB host
   would leave unsafe RAM headroom, so repeated calls are measured as rebuilds instead.
2. **Reference feasibility:** dense BF16 may not be practical on 16 GB, but no quantized
   output can be accepted without a matching I2V reference somewhere in the harness.
3. **Stage topology is semantic:** high and low experts are not interchangeable files,
   and the stage boundary is part of the recipe.
4. **T2V is separate:** its 16-channel `WanPipeline` operation has independently
   accepted exact 640x640/81-frame target-workstation success, cancellation, and fresh
   recovery. I2V proof still says nothing about T2V quality; its broader corpus and
   dense-reference comparison remain outstanding.
5. **ConvRot operation gate:** the three Winnougan artifacts have immutable source,
   size, hash, and independently inspected header/schema facts. Existing Wan and
   UMT5 stored adapters have generic `comfy_quant/int8_tensorwise_convrot` coverage,
   but the exact three remote headers were not fed to their planners: bounded header
   retrieval was unavailable and the multi-GiB payloads were intentionally not
   downloaded. Exact-artifact planning and native dispatch therefore remain pending.
   The only available sample embeds custom attention/VSR nodes, official LightX plus
   additional unpinned LoRAs, and a different four-step operation. It is evidence
   that the files ran in Comfy, not a reproducible Engine recipe authority.

## Explicit non-goals

- Do not merge T2V and I2V artifacts or acceptance results.
- Do not treat one 14B expert as a complete model.
- Do not add further quantization formats beyond the two bounded challengers before
  the exact FP8 baseline has broader creator-value and reference qualification.
- Do not rebuild or quantize official weights at Engine runtime.
- Do not change Wan 14B I2V playback rate inside the recipe; use a downstream
  interpolation/retiming operation when a different delivery rate is wanted.
- Do not call local manual artifact presence a reproducible deployment lock.
