## Product decision

Wan 14B is operation-specific: T2V and I2V use different high/low experts and support; FLF is a third endpoint operation.

Current truth:

- exact scaled-FP8 I2V, T2V, and FLF official-template baselines are accepted **Fallback** paths on RTX 5080;
- operation-specific LightX2V branches have narrow **Experimental** lifecycle acceptance;
- community ConvRot files are cataloged/source-inspected only, not runnable or accepted;
- dense matching BF16 is operation-specific **Reference** for high-memory comparison.

## Engine proof preserved

Main retains narrow accepted outputs for active-template I2V, T2V, and FLF; exact stage split/expert/schedule/fps provenance; source and endpoint conditioning; disposable-worker exit before terminal state; materialization cancellation, empty-tree cleanup, and clean recovery; and separate LightX fingerprints.

The retained active I2V artifact is 640-square, 81 frames, 16 fps, SHA-256 `83065c74cadadcba1249ff02fd2ffbe3f4f401bf9ebd2421db9eef5c3b12f665`. This is narrow Fallback evidence, not broad creator-quality promotion. Historical 24-fps/remuxed outputs remain historical.

## Challenger and next work

ConvRot becomes runnable only after a normalized operation graph, exact expert mapping, complete markers/scales/dense exceptions, matching ComfyUI/Kitchen, positive native dispatch, zero fallback, lifecycle, and creator output. A filename, sample graph, or available kernel is insufficient.

Next: broaden I2V/T2V/FLF creator corpus and source invalidation; complete switching/cancellation cells; run matching dense BF16 references on high memory; qualify at most one exact ConvRot operation after full proof.

Stop on operation/expert substitution, graph drift, false availability, fallback, assumed fps/frames, or cancellation without observed worker cleanup.
