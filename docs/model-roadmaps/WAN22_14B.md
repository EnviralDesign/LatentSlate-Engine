## Decision

Wan 14B T2V, I2V, and FLF are distinct operation contracts with operation-specific
experts/support. Current scaled-FP8 baselines are narrow Hardware-proven
**Fallbacks**; fixed LightX modes have narrow **Experimental** evidence. Matching dense
BF16 is Reference on high-memory hardware.

These accepted paths are Engine-native stored runtimes. Engine owns typed
orchestration, expert staging, workers, cancellation, output, and provenance and calls
Kitchen/native primitives directly. No ComfyUI process or graph participates.
